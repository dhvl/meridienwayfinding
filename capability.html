Design translation

We speak both languages — architectural intent and manufacturing reality.

An architect's drawing communicates intent: dimensions, materials, finishes, fixing method. Turning that into a buildable, compliant, cost-effective product takes a team that understands both sides — what the drawing is asking for, and what it actually takes to produce it at the quality and price a project needs.

We work directly from your detail design drawings, or help develop a specification where one doesn't yet exist, then manage every step from sample to sign-off to full production.

Materials & finishes

Built in the materials your specification calls for.

High-end buildings are specified in real materials — stone, metal, leather, textile — not just laminate and vinyl. We work in the same palette as the interior design, so wayfinding reads as part of the building rather than a retrofit onto it.

Metals

Brass, bronze, stainless steel and aluminium — polished, brushed, satin, patinated or powder coated to any RAL or bespoke colour match.

Stone

Marble, granite, slate and composite stone, as veneer or solid panel, for signage integrated directly into the architectural fabric.

Leather

Wrapped, stitched or embossed leather panels and inserts, to any hide, colour and stitch specification for hospitality and premium interiors.

Fabric & textile

Upholstered, acoustic-backed or printed textile panels, colour and weave matched to interior schemes.

Acrylic & laminates

Printed, engraved or layered acrylic for illuminated and directory signage.

Tactile & braille

DDA and ADA-equivalent compliant room identification, tested to the relevant standard, in any of the above finishes.

Illuminated systems

LED-backlit and edge-lit signage where visibility and evacuation requirements demand it.

Printed graphics

Digitally printed or self-adhesive vinyl graphics, colour-matched to any brand specification.

Compliance

Documentation your facilities team can actually rely on.

Institutional buildings carry compliance requirements that generic signage suppliers often treat as an afterthought. We treat them as part of the spec.

Fire rating

Confirmed appropriate to the building classification and location within it.

DDA / accessibility

Tactile and visual contrast requirements met and documented per current standards.

Structural fixing

Fixing method engineered to match the actual wall, ceiling or substrate condition on site.
