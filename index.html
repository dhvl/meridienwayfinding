The model

A drawing goes in. A building's wayfinding comes out.

Most signage companies sell you their range and ask you to fit your project around it. We work the other way round: your architect's detail drawing — dimensions, materials, finishes, fixing method — is the brief, and everything we produce is built to match it exactly.

That means consistent visual language across every building on a site, even as different phases, contractors and years pass — because the specification lives with us, not with whoever happened to win the last contract.

We manage the full path from spec to installed sign: material sourcing, sample sign-off, compliance documentation, and site fitting — so your team has one point of contact, not five.

Capability

Materials and finishes we work in.

Wayfinding for a high-end building has to match the fabric of the building itself — not just survive next to it. We work in the same material language as the architects and interior designers we support.

Metals

Brass, bronze, stainless steel and aluminium — polished, brushed, patinated or powder coated to any RAL or matched reference.

Stone

Marble, granite and slate veneers or solid panels, for signage that reads as part of the architecture, not applied to it.

Leather

Wrapped, stitched or embossed leather panels and inserts for hospitality and premium interiors.

Fabric & textile

Upholstered, acoustic-backed or printed textile panels, colour-matched to interior schemes.

Tactile & braille

DDA and ADA-equivalent compliant room identification, in any of the above finishes.

Illuminated systems

LED-backlit and edge-lit signage where visibility requirements demand it.

Printed graphics

Digitally printed or applied vinyl graphics to any brand specification.

Fixing & substrate

Engineered to match structural and surface conditions on site.

Questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

Do you only work with universities, hospitals, airports and stadiums?

No — those four are where our compliance and durability expertise runs deepest, but we work with any institutional or commercial building with a genuine wayfinding challenge, including corporate offices, retail, hospitality, transport hubs and heritage sites.

Do you work from our own design, or do we choose from a catalogue?

Your architect's drawing is the brief. We build to your exact spec — materials, finishes, dimensions and fixing method — rather than asking you to fit a standard product range.

What materials can you work in?

Metal, stone, leather, fabric and printed graphics, plus tactile, braille and illuminated systems — matched to any RAL or reference finish.

Do you handle installation as well as manufacture?

Yes. We manage material sourcing, sample sign-off, compliance documentation and site installation, so you have one point of contact from spec to installed sign.
